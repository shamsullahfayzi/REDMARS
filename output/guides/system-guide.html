1. About this document

This guide explains what the application does, who uses which part, and how information and money move between the Clinical, Finance and HR areas. It is written for the hospital's staff, not for developers. Role-by-role instructions are in the separate Role Guides; set-up instructions are in the Administrator Guide.

2. The system at a glance

REDMARS HMIS is a web application for a single hospital. The testing instance is configured for Axon Nura Psychiatric and Addiction Treatment Hospital VERIFIED – SCREEN.

  • Languages: English, Dari and Pashto can be selected in the top bar. Detailed HR screens are currently in English only VERIFIED – CODE (per the project's own HR assessment).
  • Currency and calendar: amounts are in Afghan afghani (AFN). Finance and HR use the Shamsi calendar and Kabul time (UTC+04:30).
  • Everything is permission-controlled: each person sees only the menu items their role allows, and the server re-checks every action.
  • Nothing clinical is hard-deleted. Mistakes are corrected by marking “entered in error” or adding a new record.

5. Finance — how it works

Two separate layers of money

LayerWhereWhoWhat it does
Front-desk moneyInvoices, Collections, TillReceptionist, pharmacist, admin, managementBills patients, receives payments, discounts, refunds, reconciles the day's cash at closing
Hospital accountingFinance & accountingAccountant, finance manager, payroll officer, auditor, management (read), adminChart of accounts, fiscal years and periods, journals, suppliers and bills, bank statements, patient and panel receivables, payroll, fixed assets, reports

Front-desk billing works without the finance books. The two layers meet when finance synchronises clinical billing into accounting journals (Sync action) VERIFIED – CODE. The sync was not run on the testing instance because the books were not initialised.

Front-desk money rules

  • Receptionist and pharmacist can discount up to a ceiling set by the administrator in Settings (“Maximum discount without approval”) VERIFIED – SCREEN. Above the ceiling an administrator must approve. Every discount needs a written reason VERIFIED – CODE.
  • Refunds by the receptionist or pharmacist are allowed only the same day and before the next step has happened; otherwise an administrator is needed (R5).
  • Voiding an invoice is administrator-only.
  • A lab order's own bill is paid at the reception desk (Collections); the lab will not collect a sample until the desk has been paid VERIFIED – SCREEN (Lab page text: “Collect the sample once the desk has been paid”).
  • At closing, the receptionist or pharmacist reconciles their own till; administrators and management can see every till.

Hospital accounting — first-time initialisation

The Finance page on the testing instance showed only a Shamsi/Gregorian date converter and an “Initialize the hospital books” form VERIFIED – SCREEN. Initialisation asks for a Shamsi start date, creates the AFN chart of accounts and Shamsi fiscal years, and the start date cannot be changed afterwards. Earlier hospital transactions are not imported.

Known blocker recorded by the project team The project's own finance assessment states that unpaid patient and panel invoices from before go-live cannot yet be migrated invoice-by-invoice. A general opening journal can set totals but cannot allocate receivables to individual invoices, and period close refuses that state. The accountant/financial approver and the cutover date were still pending VERIFIED – CODE (docs/finance-roadmap-assessment.md, 27 Oct 2026).

How HR connects to Clinical

  1. An administrator creates a clinical position (such as Doctor), then the employee.
  2. From the employee record the administrator creates the login. For a doctor this also creates the linked practitioner profile and department link automatically, as the Practitioners screen states VERIFIED – SCREEN.
  3. The employee exit action disables the login and ends active sessions.
  4. The roster's “on duty” calculation is built to be reused by inpatient and emergency modules; no clinical screen showing it was visible in the menu GAP.

How HR connects to Finance

Monthly salary processing is not in HR. The HR Settings tab states that salary runs, adjustments, approvals, payments and journals use Finance & accounting so the hospital has one payroll ledger VERIFIED – SCREEN.

Manual integration Payroll employees in Finance are set up separately from HR employees; the explicit HR-employee-to-payroll link is still an open item recorded by the project team, and payroll uses manually adjusted attendance VERIFIED – CODE (docs/hr-roadmap-assessment.md). Until the link is built, someone must keep salary records in Finance consistent with HR. GAP

11. Manual steps, gaps and limits

  1. GAP HR employee and attendance data do not feed payroll automatically.
  2. GAP Pre-go-live unpaid invoices cannot yet be migrated individually into the books; period close refuses the mismatch.
  3. GAP Credential-expiry reminders have a runner but no automatic daily schedule; someone must trigger it.
  4. GAP Account creation for nurse, lab technician and pharmacist roles was not verifiable on the testing instance (Section 3).
  5. GAP Source comment for nurse sample collection (R9) is flagged in the code as probably a typo; confirm the intended rule with the hospital.
  6. GAP The same lab technician can enter and verify a result; the matrix flags this as an open question.
  7. GAP No emergency “break-glass” access exists.
  8. GAP No separate roles exist for chief physician, nursing manager, lab supervisor or department head. Do not give these people the Administrator role as a shortcut; ask for a role design first.
  9. GAP The menu's “all roles” list omits Call center, so a call-center user may not see the HR link (and cannot request leave from the menu) although the permission exists. Not tested live CODE.
  10. SMS/WhatsApp reminders are stored as outbox records; actual delivery depends on a provider connection that was not seen configured.
  11. PDF output of finance reports uses the browser's “Save as PDF”.
  12. Official report formats, tax and donor templates have not been accepted by the hospital's accountant.

12. Rules that apply system-wide

RulePlain-language meaning
R1Every clinical read is recorded. Nobody is blocked from a patient in front of them; they are accountable instead.
R2Administrators can read clinical data (recorded) but can never write it. Psychiatric notes are hidden even from administrators.
R3Doctors may read full history without approval, and it is logged.
R4Nothing is hard-deleted; corrections are new records.
R5Cancel, reassign, refund, return and prescription-cancel are allowed same-day and before the next step; later needs an administrator, with a reason.
R6Pharmacist sees drugs and allergies only.
R7Nurse sees vitals, allergies and the drug list, not notes.
R8Reads are limited to the task (reception sees test names and prices, not clinical reasons).
R9Pharmacy and lab may propose catalogue changes; the administrator approves.
R10Discounts above the configured ceiling need administrator approval, with a reason.
R11Bulk export is administrator-only, needs a reason, and is heavily audited.
R12The pharmacist sees only pharmacy bills.
R13Call center sees the recall list and nothing clinical.

Source: the project's roles-and-permissions document and permission matrix. Rules describe design intent; this guide did not test each rule by signing in as each role.
