## Important exceptions to the example setup order

A separate configurable doctor schedule/fee master is not established by the current schema: Appointments support timed booking and overlap checks; consultation fees are Service fees. A Queue row is a Visit, not evidence of a separate queue-counter master. Till reporting groups Payment records by receiving user/date; the check-in method does not require opening a cash-session record first. Do not invent these prerequisites. Sources: `apps/api/src/modules/visit/visit.service.ts:129`, `apps/api/src/modules/reception/reception.service.ts:57`, `apps/api/src/modules/till/till.service.ts:35`, `apps/api/src/modules/service/service.service.ts:44`.

## HR-first rule: purpose and limits

Employee records own employment, department, position, salary metadata, documents and credentials. User records own login and permissions. Practitioner records own clinical routing and optional license number. Position.isClinical validates the Doctor selection; the title “Doctor” alone grants no system rights. The account transaction links Employee.userId, AppUser, UserRole and PractitionerDepartment; it reuses the employee number as practitioner code. `apps/api/src/modules/staff/staff.service.ts:566`; `packages/shared/src/hr.ts:122`.

Payroll ownership is separate: a FinancePayrollEmployee is not proof of an automatic HR-to-payroll link. License data can be copied from a professional-license credential, but this account method does not require a valid unexpired license. “One person, one identity” is an operational goal with unique links, not a proven cross-system person-deduplication guarantee. `apps/api/src/modules/staff/staff.service.ts:657`; `apps/api/src/modules/finance/finance-payroll.service.ts:39`.
